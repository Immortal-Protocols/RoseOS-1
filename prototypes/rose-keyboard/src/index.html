Keyboard state

    Try
    • Tap the message field to open the keyboard. Desktop typing works too.
    • Shift: tap once for one capital, double-tap for caps lock.
    • Drag along the space bar to move the cursor; hold it, then drag, to select.
    • Hold ⌫ to repeat (it speeds up to whole words), or swipe left from ⌫ to delete a word.
    • Long-press text to select a word and get Cut / Copy / Paste.
    • Swipe down on the suggestion strip, tap ⌄, tap the conversation or press Esc to hide.
    • Swipe up from the bottom edge to raise the Rose chin with the keyboard open.
