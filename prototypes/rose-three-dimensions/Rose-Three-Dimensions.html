About this motion study

Original procedural WebGL interpretation of your six keyframes. Connection, microphone and voice remain independent, including speaking while muted. Speech is simulated; no microphone or network access is used.

The object stays still; connection changes move only its upper shell. Listening keeps light close. Speaking opens the surround with a softly varying simulated envelope. Muting changes the metal without interrupting speech. Reduced motion makes each selection immediate and static.

Pixel ratio is capped at 1.5. Animation stops when hidden. Context loss switches to a static fallback; restored contexts rebuild the renderer. These safeguards are implemented, but browser visual validation was unavailable in the authoring environment.
